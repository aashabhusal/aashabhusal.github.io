Featured Research

Comparative Analysis of Capital Structure: Privately-Owned vs Government-Owned Banks

Nabil Bank vs Rastriya Banijya Bank · Final-year BBS research · Supervised by Prof. Durga Datt Pathak

5 fiscal years analyzed
(FY 2077/78–2081/82)
≈2× capital efficiency of Nabil
(ROE ~11% vs RBB ~6%)
6 ratios examined
(D/E, CAR, ROE, ROA, EPS, CD)

Using ratio, trend, comparative, and descriptive analysis of audited annual reports, Nepal Rastra Bank directives, and Basel III disclosures, the study found that ownership shapes how a bank manages its capital. Nabil, a private bank, used higher leverage (about 7.5 to 8 times) and earned close to twice the return on a similar equity base. Rastriya Banijya Bank, a government bank, kept a larger capital cushion built from reserves and lent more carefully in line with its public role.

The study recommended that Rastriya Banijya Bank make better use of its capital through careful lending, and that Nabil build a stronger capital cushion to support its higher leverage.
