Featured work

Research

Comparative Analysis of Capital Structure: Privately-Owned vs Government-Owned Banks

Nabil Bank vs Rastriya Banijya Bank · Final-year BBS research · Supervised by Prof. Durga Datt Pathak

5 fiscal years analyzed
(FY 2077/78–2081/82)
≈2× capital efficiency of Nabil
(ROE ~11% vs RBB ~6%)
6 ratios examined
(D/E, CAR, ROE, ROA, EPS, CD)

Using ratio, trend, comparative, and descriptive analysis of audited annual reports, Nepal Rastra Bank directives, and Basel III disclosures, the study found that ownership shapes how a bank manages its capital. Nabil, a private bank, ran higher leverage (about 7.5 to 8 times) and earned close to twice the return on a similar equity base (ROE ~11% vs ~6%). Rastriya Banijya Bank, a government bank, held a thicker capital cushion built from reserves and lent more conservatively (CD ratio 60–70% vs ~84%) in line with its public role.

The study recommended that Rastriya Banijya Bank make better use of its capital through prudent lending, and that Nabil build a stronger capital cushion to support its higher leverage.
